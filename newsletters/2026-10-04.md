[S2-010](https://github.com/open-many-body-research/many-body-schrodinger/blob/17860d3df2e2202388af0ece88162107eee82bc0/claims/cards/S2-010.md), via [PR #12](https://github.com/open-many-body-research/many-body-schrodinger/pull/12), derives the actual normalized product's repulsion expectation as 5α/8. At α = Z − 5/16, its submitted theorem gives

```math
Z > \frac{20+5\sqrt{10}}{24} \approx 1.492,
\qquad E_Z \le -(Z-5/16)^2 < -5Z^2/8.
```

Within this strict charge range, it connects ground-state attainment, a one-dimensional ground eigenspace and the remaining-spectrum separator to the actual continuum spinful fermionic operator, including Z = 3/2. This supplies S2.4's Lean deliverable and formalizes the range already described by paper-level S2-005. The advance is the formal connection of classical variational mathematics, rather than discovery of a new physical formula. S2-005 remains P1 pending acceptance. The endpoint/lower charges, a narrow energy enclosure, S2.3 and Theorem T are outside the result.
