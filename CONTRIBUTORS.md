**Coverage: 1 recorded merged PR(s).** Each carries one credit, split among its human contributors. The foundation import, pre-ledger work, unrecorded merged PRs, and pending PRs are excluded. Updates arrive through maintainer-reviewed follow-up PRs; this is not a live GitHub feed.

**These percentages measure recorded accepted-PR credit only.** They do not measure effort, scientific value, ownership, or the percentage of the mathematical problem solved. A 100% share with one recorded PR means only that single tracked PR. Counts of PR participation can overlap for collaborators. Shares are rounded.
