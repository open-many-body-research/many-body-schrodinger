# Bibliography

This list covers the standard literature the repository relies on or positions itself against. When you use a result, add a precise theorem or page reference and check it against the original.

The 95 works cited in the v1.0 research notes are listed in [reading-records-bibliography.md](reading-records-bibliography.md). Every arXiv ID and DOI there resolved correctly, and the list records what was actually read (full text, sections, or abstract only). Don't cite a theorem as checked if its row says only the abstract was read.

## Operator theory and spectral foundations
- T. Kato, *Fundamental properties of Hamiltonian operators of Schrödinger type*, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. **70** (1951) 195–211.
- W. Hunziker, *On the spectra of Schrödinger multiparticle Hamiltonians*, Helv. Phys. Acta **39** (1966) 451–462.
- C. van Winter, *Theory of finite systems of particles I*, Mat.-Fys. Skr. Danske Vid. Selsk. **2** (1964).
- G. M. Zhislin, *Discussion of the spectrum of Schrödinger operators for systems of many particles*, Trudy Moskov. Mat. Obšč. **9** (1960) 81–120.
- M. Reed, B. Simon, *Methods of Modern Mathematical Physics*, Vols. I–IV, Academic Press (1972–1979).
- E. H. Lieb, *Bound on the maximum negative ionization of atoms and molecules*, Phys. Rev. A **29** (1984) 3018.

## Stability of matter
- F. J. Dyson, A. Lenard, *Stability of matter. I*, J. Math. Phys. **8** (1967) 423.
- E. H. Lieb, W. Thirring, *Bound for the kinetic energy of fermions which proves the stability of matter*, Phys. Rev. Lett. **35** (1975) 687.
- E. H. Lieb, R. Seiringer, *The Stability of Matter in Quantum Mechanics*, Cambridge Univ. Press (2010).

## Regularity, decay and eigenfunction structure

- S. Agmon, *Lectures on Exponential Decay of Solutions of Second-Order Elliptic Equations*, Princeton Univ. Press (1982).
- S. Fournais, M. Hoffmann-Ostenhof, T. Hoffmann-Ostenhof, T. Østergaard Sørensen, *Sharp regularity results for Coulombic many-electron wave functions*, Comm. Math. Phys. **255** (2005) 183–227.

- H. Yserentant, *Regularity and Approximability of Electronic Wave Functions*, Lecture Notes in Mathematics 2000, Springer (2010).
