# ERRATUM-003: results that are true but trivial, originally presented as results

- **Date:** 2026-09-11
- **Affected claims:** S4-001, S5-003, S7-002

Each item below is correct, but the pre-publication notes presented it as a substantive result. None of them should be cited as progress.

| Claim | What was presented | Why it is trivial |
|---|---|---|
| S4-001 | A lithium ground-energy enclosure | Width ≈ 2.84 Ha. The endpoints are the noninteracting energy and a single-determinant mean. |
| S5-003 | Executed "box" enclosures for H, He, Li, H₂⁺, HeH | Widths 0.24–12 Ha. |
| S5-003 | A "fast separated two-center class" solved in $O((L+p+1)^3)$ | The admissible geometries depend on the requested precision $p$. This is dissociation-limit bookkeeping, not a fast solver. |
| S7-002 | A "sharp no-go theorem" for gap-limited transfer | It rearranges $E \ge a-\eta \iff \eta \ge a-E$. |

**Theorem counts.** The notes also reported theorem counts such as "76 public theorems" and "83 theorems". Most of those are one-line scalar lemmas. This repository does not report theorem counts.
