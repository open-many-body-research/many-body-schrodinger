# Code of conduct

This is a research collaboration.

**Criticize mathematics, not people.**
- Pointing out a gap in a proof is a contribution. A rejected review is part of the process, not an insult.
- Be specific: "Step 4 uses Lemma 2 outside its hypothesis r > 0" is useful, "this is wrong" is not.

**Credit work accurately.**
- Cite the sources you build on, including earlier contributions in this repository.
- Don't present someone else's result, or an AI system's output you haven't checked, as your own verified work.

**Be honest about evidence.** Don't overstate what a result shows. Understating it is fine.

**No harassment.** That includes personal attacks, discriminatory language, sexualized content, doxxing, and sustained disruption. People may take part under a pseudonym, and their privacy should be respected.

**Enforcement.** Maintainers may hide comments, lock threads, or block accounts that break these rules. You can report a problem privately through GitHub's "Report content" feature, or by opening a minimal issue that asks a maintainer to contact you.
