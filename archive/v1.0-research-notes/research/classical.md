# Classical electronic-structure methods: primary-source audit

Search window: 2024-09-09 through 2026-09-09. Research performed 2026-09-09 UTC. This is a representative, targeted search, not a claim of exhaustive coverage. Primary arXiv HTML/PDF, author manuscripts, and journal pages were inspected. Older foundational results are explicitly identified. None of the literature results below was Lean-checked in this subtask.

## Short map

| Method | PROVEN (paper proof only) | EMPIRICAL / not a general guarantee | Boundary for the requested theorem |
|---|---|---|---|
| FCI / selected CI | A Hermitian matrix restricted to a specified determinant space has a smallest eigenvalue; Rayleigh–Ritz gives an upper bound for a conforming subspace. | Sparse selection and extrapolation can approach finite-basis FCI at vastly reduced cost on examples. | The orbital basis and determinant space remain explicit approximations; no general polynomial-size selection or certified continuum error follows. |
| FCIQMC | Conditional convergence results exist for finite-dimensional inexact power iteration. | Walker compression, initiator refinements, and time averaging deliver accurate practical results. | Projection time, overlap, population bias, statistical error, and basis error all require control. Finite samples are not exact eigenpairs. |
| DMRG / tensor networks | MPS and MPO representations define finite-dimensional variational families; exact contractions are efficient for fixed bond dimensions. | Strong benchmark performance and improvements with Clifford augmentation. | Required bond dimension, global optimization, orbital-basis error, and Hamiltonian compression are separate issues. |
| Coupled cluster | Full CC is a reparameterization of FCI in the chart where reference overlap is nonzero. Local continuum/discrete well-posedness and error estimates hold under specified hypotheses. | CCSD(T), diagnostics, and other fixed-rank truncations perform well in many chemical regimes. | A fixed excitation rank is an approximation. Neither low residual of projected equations nor a small diagnostic proves global ground-state accuracy. |

The table's first column of proof statements is elementary linear algebra or the precisely delimited theorems cited below; its negative scope statements are inferences about what these results do not establish, not new complexity lower bounds.

## Recent papers read

**FCI / selected CI.** Zhang, Wang, Lu, Li, *CDFCI: High-Performance Parallel Software for Many-Body Large-Scale Eigenvalue Problems*, [arXiv:2605.04483v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.04483v1), 2026-05-06. Read §§1–3, software cost summaries, and benchmark descriptions. The method selects coordinates using gradients of an unconstrained finite-matrix optimization problem; §2 explicitly fixes a finite spin-orbital basis, with determinant dimension binomial(K,N). The paper cites convergence results under optimization assumptions and benchmarks benzene, QUEST examples, and Hubbard models. **PROVEN (paper):** the stated finite-dimensional variational/optimization reformulation, with its nondegeneracy and negative-eigenvalue conditions. **EMPIRICAL:** the reported accuracy and speed. A per-iteration polynomial cost is not a polynomial bound on total work in N and log(1/ε); determinant support, iterations, and basis convergence remain unbounded by the requested claim.

**FCIQMC.** Safari, Anderson, Alavi, Li Manni, *FCIQMC-CASPT2 with Imaginary-Time-Averaged Wave Functions*, JCTC 21, 1029–1038 (online 2025-01-17), [DOI:10.1021/acs.jctc.4c01462](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jctc.4c01462). Read the [full primary ChemRxiv manuscript, DOI:10.26434/chemrxiv-2024-kj11f](https://chemrxiv.org/engage/api-gateway/chemrxiv/assets/orp/resource/item/6721f611f9980725cf5fb826/original/fciqmc-caspt2-with-imaginary-time-averaged-wave-functions.pdf), especially §§2–3 and conclusion; first posted 2024-11-01. Time-averaged CI vectors help stabilize higher-order reduced density matrices needed in CASPT2. Examples include nickel–iron, copper–oxygen and iron–porphyrin models, up to 26 electrons/27 orbitals. **PROVEN (algebra in paper):** expectation/variance identities explain why correlated replica products bias density estimates. **EMPIRICAL:** numerical stabilization and agreement with DMRG-CASPT2. This combines finite-active-space stochastic projection and second-order perturbation outside the active space; it is not exact treatment of all Coulomb correlation or a universal cost theorem.

**DMRG / tensor networks.** Fu, Shang, Yang, Guo, *Clifford augmented density matrix renormalization group for ab initio quantum chemistry*, [arXiv:2506.16026v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.16026v1), 2025-06-19. Read §§II–III. The ansatz is Clifford-circuit-times-MPS and the algorithm updates the Hamiltonian MPO. **EMPIRICAL:** H₂O, NH₃, C₂, N₂ and N₂ dissociation benchmarks improve over two-site DMRG at equal bond dimension. These tests use STO-3G and at most 20 spin orbitals. The implementation truncates the MPO at 10^-10 and can restart five times to escape local minima. The paper itself observes that the MPO bond dimension grows exponentially with sweeps if not compressed. This is a useful practical advance, not proof of global convergence or a polynomial bound on the bond dimension required for continuum chemical accuracy.

**DMRG implementation / journal check.** Szenes et al., *QCMaquis 4.0: Multipurpose Electronic, Vibrational, and Vibronic Structure and Dynamics Calculations with the Density Matrix Renormalization Group*, [arXiv:2505.01405](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.01405), [DOI:10.1021/acs.jpca.5c02970](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jpca.5c02970), J. Phys. Chem. A 129, 7549–7574 (2025). The publisher page and primary abstract describe electronic, vibrational, vibronic, time-dependent and transcorrelated capabilities. **EMPIRICAL/software:** this is evidence of a mature and expanding MPS/MPO ecosystem, not an exactness classification. Access note: abstract and publication metadata checked; no claim here of a full theorem audit of this long software paper.

**Coupled-cluster diagnostic.** Weflen et al., *Exploiting a Shortcoming of Coupled-Cluster Theory: The Extent of non-Hermiticity as a Diagnostic Indicator of Computational Accuracy*, [arXiv:2503.20006v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.20006v1), 2025-03-25. Read derivation and numerical discussion. The density-asymmetry diagnostic vanishes at full CC/FCI in the real finite-orbital setting; examples compare CCSD, CCSDT, CCSDTQ, including Be₂. **EMPIRICAL:** diagnostic magnitude correlates with observed error in selected systems. There is no proved uniform inequality turning a small diagnostic into a ground-energy error bound. The four-correlated-electron CCSDTQ exactness example is explicitly frozen-core and finite-basis; basis and core errors can dominate a comparison with experiment.

**Coupled-cluster rigorous recent revision.** Hassan and Maday, *Analysis of the Single Reference Coupled Cluster Method for Electronic Structure Calculations: The Discrete Coupled Cluster Equations*, [arXiv:2311.00637v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.00637v3), revised 2025-12-02; original submission 2023-11-01. Read §2, §4.2 discussion, and Theorem 35/proof in §4.3. **PROVEN (paper only):** local well-posedness, quasi-optimality and residual-based error bounds for sufficiently fine discretization, under Assumptions A.I–A.III and either B.I or B.II. The estimates concern a local neighborhood of the root corresponding to the ground state. The truncated branch requires a molecule-dependent operator-norm smallness hypothesis; §4.2 explicitly says it cannot be guaranteed for arbitrary molecules even asymptotically. The residual bound contains the norm of an inverse infinite-dimensional derivative. Thus it is substantial continuum-aware numerical analysis, but does not certify that an arbitrary converged CC computation found that neighborhood, or give a uniform polynomial-cost solver. The exposition omits spin variables and assumes total nuclear charge > N−1.

**Coupled-cluster regularity.** Beck and Stamm, *On the Regularity and Interpolation of Coupled Cluster Amplitudes in Canonical Orbital Basis*, [arXiv:2605.22584v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.22584v1), 2026-05-21. Read §3, especially Theorem 3.8 and its proof. It establishes local analyticity of amplitudes given analytic molecular orbitals and a specified nondegenerate CC root, via analytic implicit-function arguments; numerical interpolation is tested. **PROVEN (paper, conditional):** local regularity of selected roots. **EMPIRICAL:** interpolation quality. Neither global root selection, continuum exactness of a truncation, nor a total polynomial bound follows. Source audit flag: Theorem 3.2 as rendered omits the analyticity hypothesis on B; it cannot literally be true for an arbitrary positive-definite matrix-valued function. I do not use that statement as a premise.

**Recent accuracy/certification audit.** Upadhyay et al., *Definitive Assessment of the Accuracy, Variationality, and Convergence of Relativistic Coupled Cluster and Density Matrix Renormalization Group in 100-Orbital Space*, [arXiv:2604.02144v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.02144v1), 2026-04-02. Read §2. This is relativistic finite-active-space work, outside the user's exact Hamiltonian. Equation (4) uses the conditional bound |δE| ≤ ||r||²/(E₁−Ẽ₀), with the exact excited energy E₁ unknown. Text allows an approximate/experimental gap surrogate; such a surrogate is not automatically a certified lower bound. Equation (5) substitutes Ẽ₁−||r₁|| for E₁ without an eigenvalue-index certification stated in that section. **PROVEN (conditional mathematics):** residual plus a true certified separating gap can bound an identified eigenvalue. **EMPIRICAL / unverified certification step:** I have not verified a complete rigorous certificate for the reported numerical instances from this section. Do not repeat the abstract's unconditional-sounding certification language.

## Older foundational primary results

**FCIQMC convergence has mathematical theorems.** Lu and Wang, *The Full Configuration Interaction Quantum Monte Carlo Method through the Lens of Inexact Power Iteration*, SIAM J. Sci. Comput. 42, B1–B29 (2020), [DOI:10.1137/18M1166626](https://doi.org/10.1137/18M1166626), [arXiv:1711.09153](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1711.09153). Read assumptions and Theorems 1–2. For a finite symmetric iteration matrix with separated leading eigenvalue, nonzero starting overlap and specified error moment conditions, the authors derive probabilistic convergence bounds. Their resource parameter depends on inverse accuracy, overlap, the eigenvalue ratio and a power of ||A||₁/||A||₂. **PROVEN (paper only):** a conditional finite-matrix convergence theorem. It would be inaccurate to say FCIQMC has no rigorous analysis; equally inaccurate to present this as polynomial in N and log(1/ε) for all Coulomb systems. Stochastic sample complexity is explicitly accuracy-dependent.

**Full continuum CC.** Hassan, Maday and Wang, *Analysis of the single reference coupled cluster method for electronic structure calculations: the full-coupled cluster equations*, Numer. Math. 155, 121–173 (2023), [DOI:10.1007/s00211-023-01371-x](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00211-023-01371-x). Primary journal text checked. **PROVEN (paper only):** invertibility/inf-sup-based local analysis of continuous CC and full CC discretizations improves on earlier small-amplitude arguments. A simple target eigenvalue and nonzero reference overlap matter; fine enough discretization and discrete stability are separate steps. This is an older foundational result, not a 2025 publication (the page displays later related-article dates).

## Mathematical distinctions that directly address the gap

1. **A complete finite-basis correlation calculation is still a compression of the continuum problem.** If V is a conforming finite-dimensional subspace of the form domain and E_V=min_{ψ∈V,||ψ||=1} q(ψ), then E₀≤E_V. Equality requires an exact ground state to lie in V (when the minimum E₀ is attained). A conforming form-core sequence gives E_V↓E₀, but this bare convergence statement has no effective rate and no finite stopping certificate.
2. **Small Ritz residuals cannot identify the lowest eigenvalue.** Independently of any paper, take A=diag(0,1,10,11) and the trial space span(e₃,e₄). Its Ritz eigenvalues are 10 and 11 and both full-space residuals vanish. A bound using their apparent gap would incorrectly certify the ground energy as 10, although min spec(A)=0. The spectral theorem only says a residual locates some spectrum; an eigenvalue-index or exclusion argument is additional information.
3. **Exact full CC is not an efficient closed form.** In a finite determinant space, if a reference determinant Φ has nonzero overlap with Ψ, intermediate-normalize Ψ=(1+C)Φ. The excitation algebra is nilpotent. The finite logarithm T=log(1+C)=Σ_{k=1}^N(−1)^{k+1}C^k/k gives e^TΦ=Ψ. This algebraic coordinate change neither discovers C nor reduces the worst-case number of amplitudes. Restricting T to low excitation ranks changes the representable states.
4. **Good energy does not by itself certify observables.** For a normalized state ψ and an isolated simple ground state with certified gap Δ>0, E(ψ)−E₀≥Δ(1−|〈ψ₀,ψ〉|²). For a bounded observable A this gives an error estimate after fixing phase. Without a positive gap or a choice inside a degenerate ground space, energy accuracy alone does not uniquely specify ground-state observables. Unbounded observables need stronger hypotheses.

These four observations are elementary mathematical deductions supplied for the parent report, not claims of novel literature results. They are not Lean-checked here.

## Sharp open gap and precise candidate question

The sharp issue exposed by the reviewed work is a **computable, two-sided certificate for the continuum Coulomb ground energy at the cost of accurate practical calculations**, including ground-state identification and basis truncation. Variational upper bounds, projected residuals, statistical error bars, and agreement between methods address different pieces; none alone supplies that certificate. This is not a proof that no such certificate can exist for restricted chemical families.

A precise research target is: define a finitely encoded class C of Coulomb molecular inputs (including charge, separation, input-bit and binding promises), and construct a terminating algorithm that returns rational L≤inf spec(H)≤U with U−L≤ε together with a checkable proof, with a stated uniform cost bound in the full input length and log(1/ε). To obtain user option (c), one must additionally prove an exact necessary-and-sufficient characterization of the classes satisfying the desired complexity bound; the sources above do not do so.

## Temptations explicitly resisted

- Reading “FCI” as continuum exactness: retained the explicit orbital subspace.
- Reading finite-basis variationality as a two-sided continuum certificate: kept only the upper bound.
- Reading sample averages as exact arithmetic: separated statistical and initiator/population errors.
- Reading low-rank representability as efficient global optimization: kept those as distinct tasks.
- Reading local CC well-posedness as global root selection: stated the local neighborhood and stability hypotheses.
- Reading diagnostic correlation as a universal error inequality: classified it empirical.
- Accepting an estimated gap as certified: checked the gap hypothesis and supplied a counterexample to the missing-index inference.
- Dating papers from a crawled PDF or related-article timestamp: checked arXiv history and publisher metadata; recent revisions are labeled separately.
- Calling all 2026 “rigorous” claims established: flagged the assumptions/steps not verified from the inspected primary text.
