# Neural electronic wavefunctions: verified research map

Search window: 2024-09-09 through 2026-09-09 (UTC); searched on 2026-09-09. This is a targeted primary-source audit, not a claim to exhaust all papers in the window. Original FermiNet, PauliNet, Psiformer and LapNet papers are older; they are identified separately rather than relabeled as recent work. No result in this file is Lean-checked.

## The certificate gap, stated without empirical language

PROVEN (paper proof only): for self-adjoint lower-semibounded H and a normalized admissible trial vector ψ, its exact Rayleigh quotient satisfies E(ψ) ≥ inf spec(H). A Monte Carlo sample mean is an estimator of this quotient, not itself a deterministic upper bound. Antisymmetry is an algebraic condition on the ansatz; it does not imply that the ansatz is an eigenvector or a global minimizer.

PROVEN (paper proof only): a zero residual or zero energy variance identifies an eigenstate, not necessarily a ground state. For H = diag(0,1), ψ = (0,1) has norm one, E(ψ)=1, and variance zero, while E0=0. Similarly, if a fixed-phase minimizer has the wrong phase, the amplitude optimization gap vanishes without eliminating E_FP−E0. The latter distinction is explicitly retained by the July 2026 Fermi Sets paper, Section II.2, equations (7)–(8).

PROVEN (paper proof only): uniform function approximation on compact sets does not by itself control kinetic energy. On [0,2π], f_k(x)=sin(kx)/sqrt(k) tends uniformly to zero, but ∫|f'_k|² dx = πk. Thus a C0 universality theorem alone cannot be substituted for Sobolev/form-norm approximation plus control of tails for unbounded Coulomb operators. This example is elementary analysis; it is not a criticism of the narrower universality theorem actually stated.

EMPIRICAL: FiRE's observed convergence law, Orbformer's chemical accuracy, cross-system transfer and diminishing DMC improvements are useful evidence. They do not provide the missing system-uniform bounds on representation size, optimizer iterations, sample mixing, quadrature/statistical uncertainty and spectral identification.

CONJECTURED / OPEN: specified physically restricted molecular families may admit compact approximants with efficiently findable parameters and affordable rigorous certificates. No such uniform theorem for general Coulomb molecules was found in the verified papers. This search result is not a proof that no such theorem exists.

## Primary-text reading and verification ledger

The full-text HTML documents were opened and the following parts inspected directly (rather than relying on search summaries):

- [FiRE full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.06087v1): introduction/per-step complexity, methods headings, Appendix J (theoretical VMC convergence), Appendix K (reference calculations), Appendix L (hyperparameters). Appendix J distinguishes convergence to stationary points from energy error and says boundedness/mixing assumptions may fail. Its Polyak–Łojasiewicz hypothesis is explicitly unestablished here.
- [Orbformer full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.19960v1): abstract/introduction, Appendix C.1 (composability, geometric invariance, spin assignment, limitations), structure of sampling and optimization appendices. Did not independently audit every benchmark table or training run.
- [Transferable solids journal full text](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43588-025-00872-z): publication metadata, abstract, main text and hydrogen-chain results; periodic boundary conditions and finite-size extrapolation are explicit.
- [Fermi Sets full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.02508v2): representation, signature encoders, equations (1)–(12) and compact-domain assumptions. The dependence of total approximation complexity on target accuracy is not supplied by the stated core-count result.
- [Deterministic NQS full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.21310v2): Sections 2.1–2.4, especially distinct energy functionals, gradient consistency and post-hoc projected diagonalization diagnostic. Its total corrected energy is not asserted to be a variational upper bound.
- [Large Electron Model full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.02346v1): abstract/introduction and scope; the tested physical system is a 2D harmonic trap.
- [Fermi Sets DMC full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.25872v1): Sections I–II.2, equations (7)–(8), explicit amplitude/phase separation. Statistical/time-step/population effects in numerical DMC are additional to the ideal exact fixed-phase variational statement.

Bibliographic metadata and abstracts were checked for FermiNet, PauliNet, Psiformer. LapNet metadata was verified from the authors' repository but its journal full text was unavailable through this browser; accordingly no detailed theorem from it is used. Extra recent searches included 2026/2608/2609 with neural-wavefunction/electronic keywords; no exhaustive coverage or “latest paper” claim is made.

Other recent primary items located but not used as load-bearing evidence: [WF-Bench, arXiv:2605.29683](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29683) (empirical fidelity scaling); [QMCTorch, arXiv:2506.09743](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.09743) (software/molecular force benchmarks); [mode connectivity, arXiv:2601.06939](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.06939) (six-electron quantum-dot optimization evidence). No conclusion here depends on uninspected proofs from those papers.
