# Root review of separated two-center computation

PASS for the exact stated input class and paper-supported implementation. Reviewed the entire producer separated_two_center_fast_v1.py SHA-256 391622bd03048eb9240cf3d453fdef0d5d459461f475c5e0552e755e290f1b62, final paper SEPARATED_TWO_CENTER_FAST_v1.md SHA-256 4bae851c4168a359f6ead964f4fa71beeed8e7dac6ac8ece7a3d00a120bee899, and the independent review in logs/reproduction/two_center_fastpath_review_v1/INDEPENDENT_REVIEW_v1.md.

For one spin-half electron and exactly two distinct rational nuclei with positive rational charges, the electronic energy satisfies [-zmax²/2-W,-zmax²/2], where W=zmax/dlo+1/(2dlo²) and 0<dlo<=half-distance. The normalized-distance partition proof is sound: S=rL²+rR²>=2d², the opposite attraction is at most zmax(rL+rR)/S<=zmax/d, and the sum of squared cutoff gradients is at most (rL+rR)²/S²<=1/d². The globally Lipschitz cutoffs preserve H1; translated Hardy controls the remaining potential terms. The stronger-nucleus exponential gives the upper bound. Molecular operator/form identification remains a paper dependency.

The exact logarithm calculation uses numerator/denominator bit lengths and one correction; negative exponents use floor division correctly. The witness dlo²<=q<4dlo² implies d/2<dlo<=d. ACCEPTED is exactly W<=2^-p. Inputs reject compressed exponent syntax, floats and booleans; the explicit arbitrary-size integer conversion policy is applied before parsing and output. Outputs use exclusive creation. The fixed count of rational operations gives O((L+p+1)^3) schoolbook bit cost, including operand size and decimal conversion. Acceptance implies p<=bit_length(numerator(q))=O(L), so accepted inputs also have polynomial ordinary encoding-length cost. A fixed geometry eventually declines as precision grows: this does not prove a fast arbitrary-precision solver for fixed molecules.

Independent integer-pair checker evidence covers four actual certificates through p=131072, 25 source-only cases, 19 rejected certificate mutations, malformed inputs and output preservation. Root inspected that review and its mathematical argument; it did not independently rerun those tests or inspect every checker line. Python semantics and the paper continuum proof are additional trust, not Lean kernel verification. Nuclear repulsion is excluded. No reference digits serve as an oracle.
