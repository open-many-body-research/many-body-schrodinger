# R24 classical rectangular L2 point evaluation

TheoremT.Continuum.tensor_box7_pointwise_four_three_bound proves, on the actual seven-coordinate closed rectangular box with first four side lengths Ly>0 and final three side lengths Lt>0:

norm(D_empty x) <= ((sqrt(Ly))^(-1)+sqrt(Ly))^4 * ((sqrt(Lt))^(-1)+sqrt(Lt))^3 * M.

The 128 fields D_s, s : Finset (Fin 7), are continuous on the box and satisfy actual HasDerivAt along each coordinate line: differentiating D_s in coordinate i outside s gives D_(insert i s). For each subset s the square root of the actual restricted Lebesgue integral of norm(D_s)^2 is at most M, with M>=0. The point x belongs to the actual closed box; endpoints are included. No pointwise estimate is assumed, and the geometric constant depends only on interval lengths, before the fields, L2 budget, point, center or any prospective base derivative order.

The proof is actual interval FTC followed by positive finite tensor averaging, exact product-measure normalization, and actual L2 Cauchy-Schwarz. Compact-box continuity supplies integrability and norm-square integrability before MemLp2 is used. The general bound has constant inverse_sqrt(product_i Li)*product_i(1+Li), proved equal to product_i(inverse_sqrt(Li)+sqrt(Li)). The four/three result rewrites this identity using the exact Fin7 split i.val<4. The named-constant and arbitrary-positive-length product corollaries are included.
