# Actual seven-coordinate tensor FTC bound

TensorBoxPointwiseFTC_v1 proves point evaluation on the actual closed product box of seven nondegenerate real intervals. The 128 fields are indexed by Finset (Fin 7), each continuous on the box; genuine HasDerivAt along coordinate lines links D_s to D_(insert i s) for i outside s. The theorem derives the one-coordinate averaging inequality internally from the actual interval FTC theorem and iterates it through product integration. It concludes that enorm(D_empty x) is bounded by the sum over all 128 subsets of the product of their side lengths times the normalized product integral of the corresponding field norm. Its uniform-average corollary has coefficient product_i (1+L_i). The constant has no derivative order or function parameter.

The box includes endpoints; all interval lengths must be positive. Indicator fields are measurable from ContinuousOn and closedness. Coordinate updates preserve the box, and the indicators equal the original norms almost everywhere under the actual interval-restricted measure. The coordinate inequality outside the box follows from the zero indicator. Product probability measures are obtained from the proven actual interval normalization. No pointwise evaluation inequality is an input.
