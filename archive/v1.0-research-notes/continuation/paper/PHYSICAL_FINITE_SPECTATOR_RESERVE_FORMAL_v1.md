# Physical finite spectator reserve

Fix a compact subset K of the actual nuclear or pair KS coefficient patch, charge Z, energy E and number n of stages. The formal theorem chooses K0≥1 before epsilon, source amplitude and raw solution. For nuclear charts the principal Grushin coefficient is exactly 4; for pair charts it is exactly 1. The operator coefficient is epsilon times b_epsilon, while the forcing is b_epsilon smul (-a0).

Given n actual cutoff stages, a measurable initial region Omega0 inside K, epsilon in [0,1], a raw solution f with region L² budget Winit and the genuine undifferentiated compact-test weak equation, the theorem constructs SpectatorFiniteState(Omega_n,f,n,R_n). The source budget is H0=K0² norm(a0)² volume(Omega0); all required coefficient and forcing words through n−1, and their weak links, are proved rather than supplied. No derivative of f is an input. R_n is the previously displayed explicit real recurrence.

At n=12 the state contains actual spectator words through order 12 and retained first-Y and ordered second-Y L² witnesses for all processed words through spectator order 11. This includes the reserve required for total order 12. Each word or its displayed Y/YY finite sum has the common budget; an aggregate over all spectator words has not been asserted. The terminal region is exactly the output of the n supplied stages. With the two-gap schedule, twelve stages consume 24 gaps, leaving room for higher-Y recovery.

The additional arithmetic lemmas prove exact joint homogeneity R_n(r H,r W)=r R_n(H,W) and monotonicity in H,W under the finite cutoff nonnegativity assumptions. Thus H≤r Hbase and W≤r Wbase imply the same actual finite state with budget r R_n(Hbase,Wbase). No division by r is needed, including at r=0. Restricting the region or derivative order and increasing the budget preserves all actual witnesses.

Evidence: three new final modules, eleven exact declarations, joint strict audit with only standard foundational axioms. These results remain conditional on the displayed raw weak equation, initial L² budget, actual cutoff geometry and compact-patch containment. The physical eigenfunction/amplitude and scheduled-box instantiations are separate ongoing work. This is not full isotropic H12, a factorial bound, an executable derivative algorithm, or full Theorem T.
