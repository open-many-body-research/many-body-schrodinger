# Actual compact weak Grushin radial estimates

For the ordinary product of four Euclidean Y coordinates and any finite Euclidean spectator space, the new theorem uses genuine global first and ordered second weak L2 derivatives of f, compact AE support K, and the actual compact-test equation P_c f=h. For c≥0 it proves, with E=||h||²:

- 16c ||D_Tj f||² ≤ E.
- ||D_Yj D_Yi f||² ≤ 3E/2.
- 2c || |y| D_Tj D_Yi f||² ≤ 3E/2.
- c² || |y|² D_Tj D_Ti f||² ≤ 3E/2 for every ordered spectator pair.

The derivative fields preserve the original closed support K. Every continuous real weight times each true first or second jet is proved to belong to L2, so no zero-by-convention nonintegrable Bochner integral supplies a bound. Existing smooth compact H2 approximation and continuous compact-weight L2 limits prove the component estimates. The approximants may use a larger compact set L; no physical radius is inferred from L. Original support radii can be imposed afterward on the actual limiting jets.

These are fully compiled mathematical implications with genuine weak H2 hypotheses. They do not prove higher regularity, factorial growth, or full Theorem T. Useful spectator norm division needs c>0. The all-pair TT estimate is weighted and does not control unweighted TT at y=0.
