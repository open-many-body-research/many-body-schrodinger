# Weak cutoff calculus in arbitrary finite Euclidean dimension

The four modules prove linearity of actual weak directional L² derivatives, the bounded smooth real multiplier product rule, an explicit first and ordered-second derivative jet for a compact smooth cutoff, and its exact Laplacian trace in any orthonormal basis.

For genuine weak derivatives D_v f=d_v and D_w d_v=e_vw, the constructed L² functions represent χf, χd_v+(D_vχ)f, and χe_vw+(D_wχ)d_v+(D_vχ)d_w+(D_wD_vχ)f. The traced formula is Δ(χf)=χΔf+2Σ_i(D_iχ)d_i+(Δχ)f. The statements use all real compact smooth integration-by-parts tests and actual Lebesgue L² quotients. Compact support of χ supplies boundedness of every coefficient required through order two; no boundedness of f is assumed.

This is preservation and explicit calculus of supplied weak derivatives. It does not prove that an L² function with an L² local Laplacian already has first or second derivatives. That separate elliptic bootstrap remains on the active path. The analytic L² maps are noncomputable definitions, not numerical integration procedures.
