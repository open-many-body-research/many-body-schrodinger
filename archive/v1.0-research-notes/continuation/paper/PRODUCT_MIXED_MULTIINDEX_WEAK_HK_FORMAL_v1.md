# Actual weak derivatives indexed by ordinary multiindices

For an open region in R⁴×R³, a genuine finite coordinate weak jet family through order m produces a family F(α,β), where α∈N⁴ and β∈N³ are the ordinary counts of Y and spectator derivatives. Its zero member is f; each member with |α|+|β|≤m has the same per-member squared L² budget W as the original word family. Whenever |α|+|β|<m, the actual local weak derivative in Y_i is F(α+e_i,β), and that in T_j is F(α,β+e_j).

The combinatorial source preserves the previous canonical seven-coordinate word representation. It proves the exact coordinate counts, total length, zero word, and that adding a single multiindex unit gives a word permutation of the corresponding prefixed old word. Smooth test-word permutation is proved from equality of ordinary second derivatives. The finite weak test identities and distributional uniqueness then identify the permuted weak representatives locally almost everywhere. This is the step connecting the canonical multiindex shift to an actual derivative; no commutation assumption or pointwise identification of arbitrary representatives is made.

The theorem `coordinateWeakHk_mixed_multiIndex` performs this construction with unchanged W. The theorem `local_smooth_weak_grushin_mixed_multiIndex_regularity` composes it with the raw local smooth equation result. For c>0 and locally smooth real B and complex s, a raw locally L² solution of (−Δ_Y−c|Y|²Δ_T+B)f=s therefore has such a finite multiindex family on each positive strictly inner rectangular box with outer closed box contained in the open equation domain. No input solution derivatives or higher differentiated equation is assumed.

The result is a per-order finite weak family. It does not assert a common family for all orders, an aggregate Sobolev norm bounded by W, a conventional/library Sobolev equivalence, a pointwise smooth representative, or uniform/factorial growth. Choosing representatives and finite bounds is classical analytic selection, not a certified executable procedure.
