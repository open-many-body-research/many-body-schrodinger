# Two-level mixed weak-jet extension

The audited formal algebra extends a mixed triangular state from Y length r to r+2, retaining total order m. Its explicit inputs are genuine first/second Y gains for predecessor words of Y length r−1 or r and total length at most m−2, plus a common output norm bound. The extension itself supplies no analytic gain: the next composition must discharge these inputs with the actual Y equation and the already proved quantitative recovery theorem.

At the odd boundary, the new derivative of length r+1 is a second derivative of a predecessor of length r−1. The old first derivative and the recovered first derivative agree almost everywhere on the open region, by local distributional uniqueness. This attaches the new second derivative to the old family. At length r+2, the same uniqueness argument attaches a second recovery to the just-constructed family. All spectator chains then follow by weak mixed commutation. Thus no unavailable total order m+1 is requested.
