# Finite coordinate weak regularity: abstract geometric composition

For any finite spectator dimension and any natural order m, the proved theorem composes m actual spectator gains and m actual two-Y-level gains. It starts with the raw local weak equation (P_c+B)f=S₀₀, c>0, and a region L² budget for f. It returns a finite common per-word squared L² budget and genuine local weak coordinate chains through order m on the final region.

This version explicitly takes two compatible finite cutoff schedules, smooth B, actual finite source weak chains, and finite source/coefficient bounds. It does not assume any input solution derivatives, higher solution equation, or successful intermediate gain. The source bounds through order m are a convenient sufficient input; individual gain theorems consume lower orders. The final larger Y reserve 2+2m is restricted to m and all interleaved coordinate words are obtained by the previously proved compatibility bridge.
