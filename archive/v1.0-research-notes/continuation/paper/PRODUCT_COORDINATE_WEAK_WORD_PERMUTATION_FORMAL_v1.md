# Permuting finite coordinate weak derivative words

On any open product domain R⁴×R^d, a finite coordinate weak jet family with actual L² budgets has locally almost-everywhere equal representatives for any two permutation-equivalent words within its order reserve. Smooth test-word expressions agree exactly, by symmetry of the ordinary second derivative and induction on list permutations. Their genuine integral weak identities then agree, and distributional uniqueness identifies the representatives locally almost everywhere.

Consequently, if v is a permutation of i::u and the order reserve exceeds |u|, the actual weak derivative in coordinate i of the representative indexed by u is the representative indexed by v. There is no pointwise equality claim for arbitrary representatives. Openness, local L², finite reserve, and all original weak chains are explicit hypotheses. This is the bridge for canonical multiindex derivative shifts, not an estimate for an unconstructed family.
