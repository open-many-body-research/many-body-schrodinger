# Actual finite spectator derivative gain

For c>0, an open Omega, and a real smooth compact cutoff chi supported in Omega and equal to one on V, there are a compact K contained in Omega and C>=0 chosen before B, the order m, the solution or source data. Suppose B is real smooth on Omega; raw locally L2 word families G_w,F_w through order m are genuine weak spectator derivative chains, and only the empty-word equation (P_c+B)G_empty=F_empty is assumed.

The formal endpoint constructs a family Gnext through order m+1 on V. For every word of length at most m, Gnext_w equals G_w as an actual raw function. All local L2 and derivative identities through the extended order are proved. Each new word j::w, |w|=m, is represented by an actual global L2 class d_wj, and

sum_j ||d_wj||² <= C/(16c) [ integral_K ||G_w||² + integral_K ||F_w-fullLeibniz(B,G,w)||² ].

The explicit finite weak-PDE calculus first derives the top-word equation with its proper lower-order commutator. The existing rough Grushin one-step gain then constructs derivatives of chi G_w, and genuine compact-test locality transfers those derivatives to G_w on V. Elementary family gluing preserves all lower words. No new top-order derivative is an input. The source derivatives through m remain substantive data; for twelve steps the last source order is eleven.

This theorem is an existence and inherited-bound statement. C is still the existing cutoff-dependent existential constant in this interface, not an evaluated H12 constant. Choice assembles witnesses and is not an executable differentiation algorithm. It does not by itself provide the twelve-domain iteration, its uniform numerical recurrence, the Y/YY reserve, H12, factorial bounds, analyticity or full Theorem T.
