# Independent review of the rational fixed-nucleus extension

Evidence: paper proof and source review, not Lean implementation verification.

Reviewed `MOLECULAR_COMPUTABILITY_v1.md`, final SHA-256
`95390dcc92a9e15d15d7959288c2732caa7a4121f0ae01e55227c95fe7fd8d7d`,
and `molecular_box_v1.py`, SHA-256
`92e26d97debed8aa698f8d1319cc19706ae210b69399bf69eb602a4bee6086ad`.
The root read the complete paper, source and imported composition adapter.

The translated integral subtracts the actual rational center and retains
all cells and signed mass groups. It does not apply invalid off-center
reflection or parity cancellations. The ceiling of total charge appears
only in conservative bounds, while the matrix retains every actual
rational charge. The translated hydrogen decomposition, two-sided
restoration argument, potential/complement bounds, extent-dependent IMS
tail, and recursive precision allocation are consistent. The separate
nuclear constant uses directed reciprocals, tests the rounded final sum,
and divides the requested total error correctly. Shared matrix assembly,
row-error and PSD primitives have the same reviewed meanings as in v2.

The stated fixed-input exponential precision cost retains nuclear input
lengths, charge denominators and separation dependence. This review does
not establish a uniform efficient solver, wavefunctions, excited states,
observables, or optimization of nuclear positions. Molecular continuum
realization and form arguments remain at paper level. Before sealing,
review requested wording that distinguish this frontier from the newly
compiled atomic self-adjointness and exact-H2 result; the final paper does
so. No mathematical or implementation defect was found in the reviewed
extension on its permitted input class.

Finite molecular stages and isolated replay are recorded separately.
They remain broad enclosures and use the disclosed Python arithmetic
runtime. Frozen and predecessor provenance is identified in the reviewed
paper; all predecessor sources and certificates remain unchanged.
