## Statement

Fix real charge `Z` and real energy `E` for the actual two-electron full-spin Coulomb Hamiltonian. Choose common constants before the original eigenstate. Every state in the original physical eigengraph has one physical locally Lipschitz representative `u` with the full-spin AE equality, fermionic permutation law, and square-sum Moser bound. The representative has Lipschitz constant at most `C_L * ‖ψ‖` on the fixed original configuration ball of radius 1.

For every spin component, either selected nuclear chart, the recovered pair chart, every unit spectator center, and every `0 < ε ≤ 1/4`, the actual KS pullback of the normalized difference `(u(εx) - u(0))/ε` has genuine all-order weak derivative families and their factorial weighted local L2 profile bound. The same pullback is smooth and real analytic on the smaller box and has factorial pointwise bounds for literal coordinate-word derivatives. Its source budget is `Csrc * ‖ψ‖`, its initialization budget is `CH12 * ‖ψ‖²`, and both profile and pointwise amplitudes are exactly linear in the original full spin-state norm.

The constants may depend on the fixed `Z,E`; they precede `ψ`, all spin configurations, selected indices, scales, centers, and derivative orders. The local geometric radius is fixed before the state. These constants are existential: the Lipschitz coefficient comes from Banach–Steinhaus and has no numerical formula or effective computation in this result.

## Premises and quantifier order

- **Assumed:** fixed real `Z,E` and the actual statement `hamiltonianGraph 2 Z ψ ((E : ℂ) • ψ)` for the original full-spin state. No charge sign, normalization, eigenvalue isolation, ground-branch or eigenspace-dimension premise is added.
- **Proved:** `M,A,C_L,Csrc,CH12` are chosen before `ψ`. `M,A ≥ 1`, `C_L` is nonnegative by its `NNReal` type, and `Csrc,CH12 ≥ 0`.
- **Proved:** a single actual `u` is chosen before all spin configurations, chart indices, centers and scales. Both endpoint predicates concern this same physical representative. Its local Lipschitz regularity, physical AE equality, permutation law and full-spin Moser bound follow from the original graph.
- **Assumed geometric inputs:** `0 < ε ≤ 1/4`, selected `i : Fin 2` for nuclear charts, and a genuine spectator position `t0` with `‖t0‖ = 1`. The original Lipschitz radius is 1 and is independent of `ψ`.
- **Proved:** the actual coefficient bounds, normalized physical weak equations, H12 initialization, source estimates, all-order weak derivatives, factorial profile, smoothness, analyticity and literal pointwise derivative estimates are derived. No input derivative, differentiated PDE, coefficient-bound, source-norm, H12, factorial or analytic premise is supplied.
- **Proved:** the estimates use the original full spin norm, without an extra factor for the number of spin configurations.

The two principal declarations separately construct witnesses; each declaration uses one common representative throughout its entire nuclear and pair family. The pointwise declaration composes the factorial declaration without changing its representative or constants. The generic amplitude and word-bound helper declarations take already-established budgets/data explicitly; the principal declarations discharge those inputs from the original graph.

## Definitions, coordinates and proof audit

`SpinSpace 2`, `Configuration 2`, `hamiltonianGraph`, the original Coulomb operator and its weak H2 graph domain remain the frozen physical definitions. The operator has kinetic coefficient `-1/2`, nuclear attraction and actual electron-pair repulsion, and the spin graph retains simultaneous spin/spatial fermionic antisymmetry.

The source uses the recovered physical nuclear KS lift and the actual isometric spectator reindexing. Its pair lift is explicitly qualified as `TheoremT.Continuum.pairKSLift`: the recovered pair convention gives original positions `T ± KS(Y)/2`, original relative position `KS(Y)`, physical pair distance `‖Y‖²`, and Grushin spectator coefficient `c = 1`. This convention is distinct from the separately encoded orthogonal Hadamard pair chart in the newer S8 stack. No operator, potential or spectator space is substituted.

The recovered initialization/factorial endpoint [`CoulombKSPhysicalFactorial_v1.lean`](../../recovery/rung2-2026-10-04/sources/CoulombKSPhysicalFactorial_v1.lean) is applied to each original scalar component graph `hgraph.2.2 σ`, the actual continuous representative, and its physical AE equality. Its supplied Lipschitz input is discharged by S8-014 at radius 1 with `L = C_L * ‖ψ‖₊`; the required scale bound becomes exactly `ε ≤ 1/4`.

Write `m = coulombMoserBoundCoefficient 2 Z E` and

`V = physicalKSUniformSourceVolume = (volume (closedBall (0 : Space (Fin 3)) 2)).toReal`.

This is the existing finite volume dominating all unit-center coefficient boxes, in the existing canonical `4+3` product space. The actual square-sum Moser bound at the origin gives

`Σσ ‖u σ 0‖² ≤ m² * ‖ψ‖²`.

The new pure helper proves that the existing full-spin source and H12 budgets are bounded by

`Csrc = M * m * sqrt V`,

`CH12 = C_H * (C_L² + m²)`.

Indeed, `spinOriginFactorialSourceBudget M u ≤ Csrc * ‖ψ‖` and `spinOriginH12Budget C_H (C_L * ‖ψ‖₊) u ≤ CH12 * ‖ψ‖²`. The recovered monotonicity theorems enlarge the actual component budgets first to the true full-spin budgets and then to these original-state bounds, retaining the same genuine weak jet families and weak derivative identities.

`PhysicalKSBoxFactorialData` records an all-order raw weak jet family equal to the literal pullback at order zero, finite local L2 budgets at each finite order, genuine Y and T weak differentiation identities on the box of radii `1/128`, and the weighted local profile bound on the box of radii `1/256`. The parameter `W` is the initialization quantity consumed in this proof; the principal predicate records its role in the factorial profile, rather than separately returning a displayed H12 witness.

The recovered pointwise conversion [`CoulombKSPhysicalPointwise_v1.lean`](../../recovery/rung2-2026-10-04/sources/CoulombKSPhysicalPointwise_v1.lean) preserves the literal continuous pullback, makes it smooth and real analytic on the box of radii `1/512`, and bounds all literal words in the four Y and three T coordinate directions. Finally, `sqrt(CH12 * ‖ψ‖²) = sqrt(CH12) * ‖ψ‖` supplies the exact amplitude homogeneity. The factorial and pointwise rates depend only on the common `M,A`, hence on the fixed physical parameters rather than the state, scale, spin or center.

## What this does not establish

The result is local to the selected physical KS charts and their normalized lifted differences. Smoothness and analyticity apply to these literal KS pullbacks; they do not assert that the original physical representative is smooth to all orders across its Coulomb cusps. The unit spectator-center condition excludes the simultaneous nuclear/pair collision at the origin.

No numerical constant algorithm, charge-uniform or energy-uniform constants, full global original-state H12 budget, complete intersection-stratum coverage, compatible unscaled physical analytic descent across every chart, uniform exterior analytic control, global dictionary approximation rate, certified solver termination/correctness, or operational bit-cost bound is proved here. This statement does not close all of Rung 2 or Theorem T.
