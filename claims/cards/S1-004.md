## 2. Plain-English statement
For one electron and a nucleus of charge $`Z>0`$, the energy form is bounded below by $`-Z^2/8`$, the 2s level, on the orthogonal complement of the ground state. Equivalently, $`H \ge -\frac{Z^2}{8} - \frac{3Z^2}{8}\lvert g\rangle\langle g\rvert`$, where $`g`$ is the normalized ground state. This is the one-electron input to the two-electron separator in [S2-002](S2-002.md).

## 3. Hypotheses
$`Z > 0`$ and scope conditions (orthogonality, smoothness of the core function). No unproved premise remains once the chain is combined; `punctured_core_rankOne_bound_extends_weakH1` takes the core inequality as a hypothesis, and `hydrogen_physical_core_rank_one` supplies it.

## 4. Definitions audit
- The weak derivatives and the $`L^2`$ space are those of [S1-001](S1-001.md).
- The half-line domain `D` is defined as the closure of the graph of compactly supported test functions (value, derivative). That makes it an $`H^1_0(0,\infty)`$-type space, defined without assuming a trace theorem.
- In the physical theorem the Coulomb term is the literal integral $`\int |g|^2/|x|`$.

## 5. What this does NOT show
The results are classical (hydrogen spectrum). No new physics.
