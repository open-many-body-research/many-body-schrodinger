## Statement

For every real charge `Z >= 2`, the actual normalized scalar two-electron Coulomb ground state has genuine ordered first and second weak derivative witnesses whose physical polynomial moments of every natural order belong to L2. For every positive exponent `a` with `a^2 < Z^2/112`, one finite prefactor precedes all moment orders `k`; the combined component norm is bounded by `C * k! / a^k`.

The conclusion concerns the literal configuration radius `||x||` of the original physical state and its actual weak derivative components. It constructs L2 representatives equal almost everywhere to `||x||^k f(x)`, `||x||^k d_i(x)`, and `||x||^k e_ij(x)`, including every ordered mixed second derivative. The state remains normalized, real almost everywhere, exchange symmetric and invariant under every real orthogonal configuration rotation, with its original weak-H2 Coulomb graph at the actual variational ground energy.

A graph consumer applies to every ground eigengraph state and every supplied actual weak derivative family, so this result can be applied to the same state selected by a separate physical ground construction. A general scalar eigenstate version takes a positive exponential L2 membership premise; the physical ground theorem proves that premise from the recovered exterior coercivity.

## Premises and definitions audit

- **Assumed principal charge range:** `2 <= Z`; the positive exponent and strict gap are explicitly quantified in the conclusion. A positive admissible exponent exists, for example `a=Z/16`.
- **Proved physical state:** the unchanged recovered `twoElectron_ground_real_symmetric_rotation_decay` supplies the normalized actual scalar Coulomb graph, realness, exchange/rotation laws and exponential L2 decay. The new theorem does not assume attainment, rotation invariance, decay or polynomial moment membership.
- **Proved weak derivative witnesses:** unpacking the actual scalar graph supplies the original `d,e` and all displayed `WeakPartial` laws. The recovered first/second derivative exponential decay theorems derive exponential L2 membership for those same components.
- **Trusted definitions:** `Configuration N`, `SpatialL2 N`, `HasH2`, `WeakPartial`, `scalarHamiltonianGraph` and `variationalGroundEnergy` are the unchanged Foundation definitions. No surrogate Hamiltonian, domain, abstract sequence or formal moment is introduced.
- **Actual norm:** the displayed square root is the sum of squares of the L2 norms of the weighted physical state and all its ordered weak derivative components.

For `t>=0` and `a>0`, the pinned exponential-series theorem gives `(a*t)^k/k! <= exp(a*t)`, hence `t^k <= (k!/a^k)exp(a*t)`. Genuine pointwise domination proves weighted L2 membership and its exact norm bound. The common `C` is the square root of the sum of squares of the exponentially weighted component norms. Squaring, summing and taking the nonnegative square root gives the displayed all-order bound. This chosen prefactor precedes `k` and depends on the actual state/derivative family and exponent.
