## Literal derivatives, rates, and neighborhoods

The imported derivative predicate uses the genuine Fréchet iterates of the literal original descended series. The complex derivative is
`iteratedFDeriv ℂ (∑ α + ∑ β)` evaluated on the canonical coordinate word with the indicated multiplicities. The real derivative is the corresponding genuine `iteratedFDeriv ℝ` of the real coordinate restriction of those same complex series. Neither derivative is a formal jet, an assumed recurrence, or a newly chosen analytic substitute.

Write `Rate=physicalKSPointwiseRate M A`, `D=32*(7*Rate)^2`, and `S=7*Rate`. On the closed quarter polydisc
`D*‖X‖_Pi≤1/4`, `S*‖T‖_Pi≤1/4`, the imported bound is

```text
Budget(M,A,F0,W,α,β)
 = 16*physicalKSPointwiseAmplitude(M,A,F0,W)
   *(24*D)^(sum α)*(24*S)^(sum β)
   * product_i (α_i!)*product_i (β_i!).
||D_X^α D_T^β A|| ≤ Budget,
||D_X^α D_T^β B|| ≤ D*Budget.
```

The dimension factor is **24**, exactly as proved by the recovered source. The factor 4 from an earlier historical paper-style estimate is not claimed. For the original-norm theorem the bound for A is `Budget(M,A,Csrc,CH12,α,β)*‖ψ‖`, and the bound for B is `D*Budget(M,A,Csrc,CH12,α,β)*‖ψ‖`. The new equality `physicalKSDescentDerivativeBudget_original_norm` derives this from the actual amplitude definition and the nonnegative H12 budget; it is not an additional physical hypothesis.

The data also retains the original pointwise factorial data, joint complex analyticity of A and B, and the actual physical Euclidean identity
`v(X,t0+T)=A(X,T)+‖X‖*B(X,T)`
on the recovered bounded neighborhood. The function v is exactly the normalized physical difference `(u(epsilon•x)-u(0))/epsilon` in the displayed physical nuclear or pair coordinates. The selected collision point is included.

The recovered pair lift uses center plus/minus half the pair separation and principal coefficient 1. It is not identified with the orthogonal Hadamard pair chart with coefficient 4 from S8-008.
