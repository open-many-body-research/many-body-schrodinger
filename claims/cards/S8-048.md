## Genuine degree and cardinality proof

Each translated coordinate polynomial X_j-C(a_j) has total degree at most one. The actual term with n ordered coordinate factors has degree at most n; multiplying by its genuine Taylor scalar coefficient does not increase the degree. Actual finite sum degree bounds therefore give totalDegree(realTaylorMvPolynomial f a N)<=N-1.

For N>0, every supported exponent has its sum of coordinate exponents at most this total degree and hence below N. For N=0 the actual Taylor polynomial and its support are zero and empty, so the supported-exponent statement and cardinality bound hold exactly. Each coordinate exponent is at most the total exponent sum.

The literal transported support is a subset of the finite coordinate box (Finset.range N)^3. Its cardinality is at most N^3 by the proved finite Pi-product cardinality, and the Finsupp equivalence is injective, so the original polynomial support has the same bound. No assumed support-size premise or coefficient cancellation estimate is used.

The exact dictionary equality transports S8-045's true derivative error for the SAME real Taylor value polynomial into the displayed finite dictionary. The proof does not replace a derivative field by an independently chosen polynomial.
