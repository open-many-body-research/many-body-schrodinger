# S2-003: Two-electron Temple enclosure with the physical separator proved

**Tier:** L (conditional on the trial data)  **Sector:** S2  **Status:** foundation  **Depends on:** S2-002

## 1. Formal statement
`TheoremT.Continuum.twoElectron_physical_directed_spectral_enclosure`, in [`TwoElectronPhysicalCertificate_v1.lean`](../../lean/Foundation/TwoElectronPhysicalCertificate_v1.lean):
```lean
theorem twoElectron_physical_directed_spectral_enclosure (Z : ℝ) (hZ : 0 < Z)
    (ψ : (coulombPartialOperator 2 Z).domain) (hψ : ‖(ψ : FermionicSpace 2)‖ = 1)
    (L U r : ℝ)
    (hL : L ≤ (inner ℂ (ψ : FermionicSpace 2) (coulombPartialOperator 2 Z ψ)).re)
    (hU : (inner ℂ (ψ : FermionicSpace 2) (coulombPartialOperator 2 Z ψ)).re ≤ U)
    (hUβ : U < -(5*Z^2/8))
    (hr : ‖coulombPartialOperator 2 Z ψ -
      ((inner ℂ (ψ : FermionicSpace 2) (coulombPartialOperator 2 Z ψ)).re : ℂ) •
        (ψ : FermionicSpace 2)‖^2 ≤ r) :
    ((L-r/(-(5*Z^2/8)-U) : ℝ) : EReal) ≤ spectralGroundEnergy 2 Z ∧
      spectralGroundEnergy 2 Z ≤ (U : EReal)
```

## 2. Plain-English statement
Take any normalized two-electron trial wavefunction in the operator domain. If its energy is certified to lie in $[L,U]$ with $U<-5Z^2/8$, and its squared residual is at most $r$, then the true ground energy lies in $[L - r/(-5Z^2/8-U),\, U]$.
