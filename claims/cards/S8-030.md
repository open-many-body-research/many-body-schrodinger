## Statement

For every real `Z >= 2`, one actual normalized scalar two-electron Coulomb ground state f, with its actual first and all ordered second weak derivative families d,e, has normalized compact H2 approximants. For every `a > 0` with `a^2 < Z^2/112`, one finite nonnegative C is chosen before every real R satisfying

`R >= physicalCompactNormalizationRadius a C = max 1 (2*C/a)`.

The approximant is literally `v_R = c_R • F_R`, where F_R is the actual S8-024 cutoff class and `c_R = ((||F_R||^(-1) : Real) : Complex)`. Its norm is exactly one, and its supplied AE representative has compact topological support inside the open ball of radius 2R. The raw cutoff has norm at least 1/2. All first, ordered second, and defect derivative families are genuine WeakPartial witnesses in the original physical Configuration 2 and SpatialL2 2.

One and the same prefactor C bounds the combined 43-component H2 defect, the actual scalar Hamiltonian eigenvalue residual, and the absolute Rayleigh error by `C*exp(-a*R)`. The Rayleigh quantity is the real part of the actual complex L2 pairing `(inner Complex v_R h_R).re`, where h_R is a proved scalarHamiltonianGraph image of v_R. No assumed normalized approximation or assumed target graph residual enters the physical endpoint.

## Quantifiers and proof audit

- The same original f,d,e are chosen before every a and R. The original f retains normalization, exchange symmetry, actual H2 domain, actual Coulomb ground graph, AE realness and full orthogonal rotation law. The conclusion does not assert exchange or rotation symmetry of the compact approximants; S8-024's chosen cutoff is used only with its proved support and derivative facts.
- C may depend on the original state, its genuine derivative family, charge and exponent, and precedes every R. Its value is existential. The displayed threshold is an explicit expression in that C and a, rather than an algorithm for computing C.
- `Real.add_one_le_exp (a*R)` and `exp(a*R)*exp(-a*R)=1` prove `C*exp(-a*R) <= 1/2` above the conservative threshold. Reverse triangle then gives `||F_R|| >= 1/2`, `||c_R|| <= 2`, and `||c_R-1|| <= 2*delta`, with delta the raw H2 defect bound.
- For every genuine component v,w, the exact identity `c_R • v-w = c_R • (v-w)+(c_R-1) • w` bounds its normalized defect by `2*delta*(1+H)`, where H is the original `physicalH2ComponentNorm f d e`. The 43 squared components yield the safe combined factor `14*(1+H)*delta`.
- If C0 is the frozen actual compact H2/residual prefactor, the proof chooses `C = 16*(1+H)*C0`. This bounds both the normalized H2 defect and the normalized residual. Since `C0 <= C`, the output threshold also implies the raw normalization threshold. There is no state- or R-dependent prefactor hidden after the universal R quantifier.
- Scalar graph multiplication derives the actual normalized graph from the raw actual graph. Its residual is exactly `c_R • (h_R-E • F_R)`, so the factor 2 controls it. The actual inner-product Cauchy-Schwarz inequality and norm-one law derive the absolute Rayleigh error from that residual.
- Complex scalar multiplication and the unchanged weak derivative subtraction law establish all normalized and defect WeakPartial clauses. Compact support is asserted for the literal supplied AE representative, not for an arbitrary representative chosen internally by Lp.
