## Exact physical claim

For every real charge Z and every positive radius r, the literal two-electron Coulomb potential restricted to the physical six-dimensional closed ball of radius 2r has actual L2 norm exactly r² times its norm on the fixed radius-two ball. Its literal open-ball restriction has derived L2 membership and norm at most that same fixed constant times r². All integrability is discharged by the original compact Hardy bounds; no integrability, homogeneity or norm-scaling premise is supplied.

Separately, the literal compact real L2 class of 1 plus the three original inverse distances has norm at most r² times its fixed radius-two norm for 0<r≤1. The pair distance and Coulomb coefficient remain original and unnormalized. These constants precede every positive radius and do not require a chosen ground state, profile or derivative estimate.

The additional 1 in the inverse-distance budget explains its separate restriction r≤1.
