## Statement

Fix real charge `Z` and real energy `E` for the original two-electron full-spin Coulomb Hamiltonian. Common regularity constants, a positive relative neighborhood width, and one finite family of unit physical spectator centers are chosen before every eigenstate.

Every original physical eigengraph state has one representative carrying its physical AE equality, fermionic permutation law, square-sum Moser bound and Lipschitz bound on the fixed ball of radius 1. At every `0 < ρ ≤ 1/4`, all spin components, both nuclear charts and the original coefficient-1 half-sum pair chart have true analytic reconstruction of this same original representative throughout their selected annular neighborhoods. On these neighborhoods, all genuine real Euclidean physical coordinate mixed derivatives of the literal unscaled A and B coefficient functions are bounded by a common amplitude times the original full spin-state norm.

For multiindices `α,β : Fin 3 → ℕ`, let `n = Σα + Σβ`, `S = 7 * physicalKSPointwiseRate M A`, `D = 32 * S²`, `m = coulombMoserBoundCoefficient 2 Z E`, and `Q(α,β) = physicalKSDescentDerivativeBudget M A Csrc CH12 α β`. The bounds are exactly

`‖∂^(α,β) A_original(p)‖ ≤ ((if n = 0 then m else 0) + ε * ε^(-n) * Q(α,β)) * ‖ψ‖`,

`‖∂^(α,β) B_original(p)‖ ≤ ε^(-n) * D * Q(α,β) * ‖ψ‖`.

The actual nuclear scale is `ε = ρ`; the original half-sum pair scale is `ε = ρ/sqrt 2`. The `u(0)` term in A is retained at order zero and vanishes from its higher derivatives. B has no additional positive power of ε. The precise Lean expression for the inverse scale is `(ε⁻¹)^n`, so there is no ambiguous natural-number subtraction in these formulas.

## Genuine physical derivatives and exact scaling

The derivative used above is the literal real iterated Frechet derivative evaluated on the canonical multiindex word of actual physical Euclidean coordinate directions:

```lean
def physicalMultiindexDeriv (f : Position × Position → ℂ)
    (α : Fin 3 ⊕ Fin 3 → ℕ) (p : Position × Position) : ℂ :=
  iteratedFDeriv ℝ (∑ i,α i) f p
    (fun j => physicalCoordinateDirection (multiindexCoordinateWord α j))
```

`physicalCoordinateDirection` consists of the Euclidean coordinate unit directions in the actual `Position × Position` space. The frozen physical complex-coordinate CLM maps each such direction to the literal corresponding `Pi.single` complex direction. Its already-proved real/complex derivative identity therefore transports the existing complex mixed derivative bound to these genuine Euclidean derivatives without identifying the Euclidean norm with the finite Pi sup norm or inserting a new dimension premise. The canonical word is a classical representative with the prescribed multiplicities; no executable word-generation algorithm is asserted.

The exact unscaled A and B derivative identities are:

```lean
theorem physical_multiindex_rescaled_collision_A
    (a : Position × Position → ℂ) (u0 : ℂ) {ε : ℝ} (hε : 0<ε)
    (p : Position × Position) (α : Fin 3 ⊕ Fin 3 → ℕ)

Analyticity supplies the local differentiability needed for that sum/scalar operation. No derivative equality is postulated.

## Remaining scope

The budgets concern all coordinate mixed derivatives of the literal physical A/B coefficient functions in the selected isolated-collision neighborhoods. They are not a new full operator-norm bound for arbitrary multilinear directions, a global derivative norm for the original cusp-containing wavefunction, or a holomorphic reconstruction in independent scalar distance variables.

The inverse powers of ε are explicit and may grow as the physical radius approaches the triple origin. No radius-uniform unscaled derivative budget or simultaneous-origin regularity follows. The covered collision-free bulk and exterior region receive no analytic estimate here. No transition between distinct collision types/selected indices, global H2 approximation or dictionary rate, physical polynomial moments, solver termination/correctness, or bit-cost bound is supplied.
