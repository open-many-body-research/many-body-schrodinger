## Statement

Fix real charge `Z` and real energy `E` for the actual two-electron full-spin Coulomb operator. Common regularity constants, a positive relative width `η < 1/16`, and a finite family of genuine unit physical spectator centers are chosen before every state.

The same finite family covers every positive configuration radius through the actual S8-016 cover by the collision-free set, both nuclear neighborhoods, and the original half-sum pair neighborhood. Every actual eigengraph state has one physical representative carrying its AE equality, full fermionic permutation law, square-sum Moser bound, and original-norm Lipschitz estimate on the fixed ball of radius 1.

For every `0 < ρ ≤ 1/4`, every spin component, every finite center and every original configuration point in any selected nuclear or pair neighborhood, the literal descended unscaled A and B functions are real analytic at the corresponding physical coordinate point and satisfy the exact identity

`u(x) = A(p(x)) + ‖p(x).1‖ * B(p(x))`.

This supplies analytic applicability throughout the selected collision neighborhoods, including their selected collision points. It goes beyond the geometric cover and beyond reconstruction only as a germ at a collision center. The collision-free part of the cover receives no analytic estimate here. The triple origin is excluded by the positive-radius cover.

## Exact physical coordinates and scaling

Let `R = physicalDescentNeighborhoodRadius M A = min (1/1024) (7 * physicalKSPointwiseRate M A)⁻¹`. The new selected-position radius is

`RX = physicalDescentPositionRadius M A = min (R²) (32 * (7 * physicalKSPointwiseRate M A)²)⁻¹`.

Both are proved positive from `A ≥ 1`. The proof chooses

`η = min (1/32) (min RX R / sqrt 2)`.

Thus `η > 0`, `η < 1/16`, and `sqrt 2 * η ≤ RX,R`. These are actual convergence/reconstruction radii of the frozen physical descent. Shrinking the geometric cover to this common width preserves the compact-sphere finite subcover.

For a selected nuclear chart, the original physical coordinate is

`p(x) = (position x i, position x (1-i))`

and the normalized-difference scale is exactly `ε = ρ`. Actual position linearity proves that the normalized deviations are bounded by `η`. The unchanged `configurationProductEquiv` and genuine spectator isometry reconstruct the original configuration from `p(x)` exactly; this is proved for either selected index.

For the original coefficient-1 pair chart, the physical coordinate is

`p(x) = (position x 0 - position x 1, (position x 0 + position x 1)/2)`.

On the unit configuration sphere the selected pair-collision center has half-sum norm `1/sqrt 2`. Consequently the correct scale is `ε = ρ/sqrt 2`. The S8-016 annular pair center `(sqrt 2)⁻¹ * t0` becomes the genuine normalized unit spectator center `t0`, and both normalized coordinate deviations are exactly `sqrt 2` times the annular deviations. The proof derives these identities from actual original positions and verifies `0 < ε ≤ 1/4` from the physical radius range.

The pair convention is explicitly `TheoremT.Continuum.pairKSLift`, with physical positions `T ± KS(Y)/2`, relative position `KS(Y)`, and cleared spectator coefficient 1. It is not the newer orthogonal Hadamard pair chart. The actual original coordinate CLM reconstructs `x` from the relative-position/half-sum coordinate exactly.

In either chart, at every neighborhood point the real physical coordinate embedding lies in the proved complex analytic domain. The recovered surjectivity theorem supplies the literal physical identity for all selected positions of norm below `RX`; no preimage or pushforward assumption is added. Positive-scale inversion gives precisely

`A_original(p) = u(0) + ε * A_scaled(p/ε)`,

`B_original(p) = B_scaled(p/ε)`.

The factor `ε * ‖p.1/ε‖ = ‖p.1‖` cancels the scale in the B term. Composing analytic functions with the genuine inverse scaling proves analyticity at each original coordinate point, and the exact coordinate reconstruction replaces `u(C(p(x)))` by `u(x)`.

The width bound also permits the existing S8-016 exclusions: each nuclear neighborhood avoids the other nucleus and pair collision, while each pair neighborhood avoids both nuclei. These results concern the actual original singularities.

## Remaining scope

The covered collision-free bulk has no analytic estimate in this statement. Analytic applicability is limited to the displayed selected collision neighborhoods and `0 < ρ ≤ 1/4`; the geometric all-radii cover does not supply exterior analytic control. The triple origin and simultaneous collision/intersection strata remain unresolved.

The coefficient functions are analytic in the genuine physical relative-position/spectator coordinates. This theorem does not construct analytic functions of independent scalar distance variables, transition compatibility between different collision types or selected indices, quantitative unscaled A/B derivative bounds, global approximation or dictionary rates, certified solver termination/correctness, or bit-cost bounds. The original-state source/H12 bounds are used to derive the normalized analytic inputs; the output reconstruction predicates do not themselves record new unscaled derivative budgets. This local finite-cover obligation does not close all of Rung 2 or Theorem T.
