## Statement

For every `a > 0` with `a^2 < Z^2/112`, one finite prefactor C is chosen before every real radius `R >= 1`, and the combined state/first/all ordered second derivative defect norm is at most `exp(-a*R)*C`.

The cutoff is one fixed genuine `ContDiffBump` on the original `Configuration 2`, with inner radius 1 and outer radius 3/2. Its scaled version is literally `χ_R(x)=χ(R⁻¹ • x)`. It equals 1 on the closed radius-R ball, is smooth, and has compact topological support inside the open ball of radius 2R. The margin is proved via the closed ball of radius 3R/2. One pair of finite nonnegative constants B1,B2 controls every physical first/ordered second coordinate derivative by B1/R and B2/R² before all state and radius choices.

The compact state is the existing actual `cutoffMul` class of `χ_R(x) f(x)`. Its first derivative is `χ_R d_k + (∂_kχ_R)f`; its ordered second derivative is `χ_R e_kl + (∂_lχ_R)d_k + (∂_kχ_R)d_l + (∂_l∂_kχ_R)f`. - The principal charge range is exactly `2 <= Z`; the exponent and its strict gap are explicit in the conclusion. The same original f,d,e precede every a and R.

- Smooth multiplication uses the unchanged Foundation distributional product rule. First and ordered second cutoff derivatives vanish on the inner open ball, so every product-rule error is controlled by the genuine exterior L2 component.
- Every individual exterior component norm is at most `weakH2ExteriorNorm f d e R`. For R >= 1, derivative factors B1/R and B2/R² are bounded by B1 and B2. Each defect component is bounded by `(1+2*B1+B2)` times that original H2 tail. The 43 squared components yield the safe combined factor `7*(1+2*B1+B2)`.
- The recovered graph-derived exponential H2 tail supplies `Ctail` with original d,e. The final C is `7*(1+2*B1+B2)*Ctail`, giving the same exponent a.
- Compact support applies to the literal displayed AE representative `χ_R f`. An arbitrary representative selected internally by Lp need not have pointwise compact support; the card does not assert that stronger representative-dependent property.
