## 1. Formal statement

The principal declaration is `ManyBody.S2.screened_ground_branch_above_threshold`, in
[`ScreenedGroundBranch.lean`](../../lean/ManyBody/S2/ScreenedGroundBranch.lean):

```lean
theorem screened_ground_branch_above_threshold (Z : ℝ)
    (hZ : (2*Real.sqrt 2 + Real.sqrt 5)/3 < Z) :
    (variationalGroundEnergy 2 Z).toReal ≤ -(Z - Real.sqrt 2 / 4)^2 ∧
    (variationalGroundEnergy 2 Z).toReal < -(5*Z^2/8) ∧
    ∃ g : (coulombPartialOperator 2 Z).domain,
      ‖(g : FermionicSpace 2)‖ = 1 ∧
      coulombPartialOperator 2 Z g =
        ((variationalGroundEnergy 2 Z).toReal : ℂ) • (g : FermionicSpace 2) ∧
      (∀ u : (coulombPartialOperator 2 Z).domain,
        coulombPartialOperator 2 Z u =
          ((variationalGroundEnergy 2 Z).toReal : ℂ) • (u : FermionicSpace 2) →
        (u : FermionicSpace 2) =
          inner ℂ (g : FermionicSpace 2) (u : FermionicSpace 2) • (g : FermionicSpace 2)) ∧
      (∀ w : (coulombPartialOperator 2 Z).domain,
        inner ℂ (g : FermionicSpace 2) (w : FermionicSpace 2) = 0 →
        -(5*Z^2/8) * ‖(w : FermionicSpace 2)‖^2 ≤
          (inner ℂ (w : FermionicSpace 2) (coulombPartialOperator 2 Z w)).re) ∧
      (∀ z ∈ TheoremT.OperatorTheory.unboundedSpectrum (coulombPartialOperator 2 Z),
        z = ((variationalGroundEnergy 2 Z).toReal : ℂ) ∨ -(5*Z^2/8) ≤ z.re)
```

## 2. Plain-English statement

For every real nuclear charge

```math
Z > Z_{\mathrm{unc}} := \frac{2\sqrt{2}+\sqrt{5}}{3} \approx 1.688,
```

the physical two-electron atom has a ground state unique up to phase. Its energy obeys

```math
E_Z \le -\left(Z-\frac{\sqrt{2}}4\right)^2 < -\frac{5Z^2}{8},
```

and every other spectral value has real part at least `-5Z^2/8`.
This improves the foundation's charge restriction `Z > sqrt(32)/3`, approximately
`1.886`, while using its established repulsion uncertainty bound.

The proof uses the actual normalized hydrogen product singlet with independently
chosen scale `alpha = Z - sqrt(2)/4`. Its exact one-body contribution is
`alpha^2 - 2*Z*alpha`. The actual repulsion expectation is retained in the energy
identity and bounded by `alpha/sqrt(2)`, giving the displayed optimized upper bound.

## 4. Definitions audit

- `FermionicSpace 2` is the closed antisymmetric subspace of continuum spinful
  `L2((R^3 x {up,down})^2)`, with simultaneous spatial and spin exchange. The trial is
  a symmetric spatial product tensored with the antisymmetric normalized spin singlet.
- `coulombPartialOperator 2 Z` is the same self-adjoint operator as [S1-001](S1-001.md),
  with domain exactly the componentwise weak `H^2` fermionic functions. Its graph is
  the untruncated electronic Coulomb Hamiltonian
  `-Delta_1/2 - Delta_2/2 - Z/r_1 - Z/r_2 + 1/r_12`.
- `hydrogenProductTrial Z alpha hAlpha` retains the physical nuclear charge `Z` in
  the operator while using normalized orbitals proportional to `exp(-alpha*r)`.
  The argument `alpha` changes the trial, not the Hamiltonian.
- `hydrogenProductRepulsion alpha hAlpha` is the continuum integral of the actual
  normalized product density times `1/r_12`. It is not a supplied or substituted scalar.
- `variationalGroundEnergy 2 Z` is the original physical variational ground energy;
  the foundation proves it finite and equal to form and spectral ground energy.
  The eigenspace-line statement identifies every domain eigenvector at that energy.
- Energies are electronic Hartree values for a fixed single nucleus. The operator
  has no nuclear repulsion constant.

## 5. What this does NOT show

- This does not solve all of open problem S2.4. The full exact-repulsion threshold
  `(20+5*sqrt(10))/24`, approximately `1.492`, requires formalizing the moment
  `5*alpha/8`; that identity is not proved or assumed here.
- It does not cover H-minus at `Z = 1`, establish the sharp critical charge, or
  replace the rank-one separator with a stronger one.
- It supplies an energy upper bound, not a narrow numerical ground-energy enclosure;
  S2.3's formal Hylleraas-moment bridge remains open.
- It proves neither the approximation rate nor the algorithm and bit-cost claims
  of Theorem T. Theorem T remains open.
- The algebra and trial are classical variational methods. The contribution is the
  checked connection to the repository's actual unbounded spinful fermionic operator.
