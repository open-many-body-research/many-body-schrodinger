# S3-002: Helium ground energy to width 8.19 × 10⁻⁹ Ha (dyadic dictionary E10)

**Tier:** C (conditional; see §3)  **Sector:** S3  **Status:** foundation  **Depends on:** S2-002, S2-003

## 1. Formal statement
$$
-2.903724385192751581540207 \;\le\; E_\text{He} \;\le\; -2.903724377006065467379698,
\qquad \text{width } 8.1867\times10^{-9}.
$$

- **Trial.** The trial space is the dictionary $E_{10}$: 336 functions $e^{-\alpha(r_1+r_2)}\,p(r_1,r_2,r_{12})$ with $\alpha = 2^{j+1}$ ($j=0,\dots,5$) and polynomial degree $10-2j$. Each function carries a rational power-of-two normalization. The trial coefficients are rational and stored in [`screen_E_10.json`](../../certificates/helium-dyadic-dictionary/screen_E_10.json).
- **Checker.** [`certify.py`](../../certificates/helium-dyadic-dictionary/certify.py) does three things:
  - it recomputes $\lVert\Phi\rVert^2$, $\langle\Phi,H\Phi\rangle$ and $\lVert H\Phi\rVert^2$ exactly by grouped polynomial $H$-actions;
  - it asserts that these equal the pairwise-matrix contractions;
  - it checks the regularized fixed-shift pencil by 1024-bit interval LDL.
- **Enclosure.** It then applies Temple's bound with $\beta=-5/2$ and rounds the endpoints outward to 24 decimal places.
- **Output.** [`certificate_E_10_1024.json`](../../certificates/helium-dyadic-dictionary/certificate_E_10_1024.json).

## 2. Plain-English statement
This is a second, independent helium enclosure. It is about 100× tighter than [S3-001](S3-001.md) and uses a basis with several exponents. The reference value lies inside, $2.8\times10^{-11}$ Ha below the upper endpoint.

## 3. Hypotheses
The hypotheses are the same as for [S3-001](S3-001.md): the separator and the Temple step are proved in Lean, and the moment formulas are paper-level (P1). At 512 bits the E10 interval LDL check failed on a pivot. At 1024 bits it passes, and the failure was recorded rather than hidden.

## 4. Definitions audit
- The operator is the same as in [S3-001](S3-001.md).
- The pencil matrices [`matrices_E_10.json`](../../certificates/helium-dyadic-dictionary/matrices_E_10.json) are inputs, but the Temple bound itself uses only the directly recomputed moments.

## 5. What this does NOT show
- It does not show the dictionary's asymptotic rate. That is the separate paper claim [S8-002](../registry.yaml).
- It is not a Lean-checked value.

## 6. Reproduce
```bash
cd certificates/helium-dyadic-dictionary && python3 certify.py E 10 --bits 1024   # about 6 minutes
```
