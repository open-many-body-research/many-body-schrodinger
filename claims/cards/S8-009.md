# S8-009: Physical nuclear KS pullbacks have full joint local weak H³

**Proposed tier:** L, kernel checked; human maintainer review pending. **Sector:** S8. **Proposed status:** proposed. This submission is not maintainer acceptance.

Every physical spinful Coulomb eigenfunction graph with positive electron number supplies one physical representative whose nuclear KS pullbacks have all ordered third weak L² derivatives locally on the actual nuclear coefficient patches. The scalar theorem starts from any fixed continuous representative of the physical scalar eigenfunction. Neither physical theorem assumes transformed derivatives or a transformed equation.

## 4. Actual derivative recurrence and assembly

[`HigherSpectatorRegularity.lean`](../../lean/ManyBody/S8/Internal/HigherSpectatorRegularity.lean) proves the actual inhomogeneous recurrence: if `(P₀+B)U=g`, `D_Tj U=d_j`, and `D_Tj g=k_j` weakly, then

```math
(P_0+B)d_j=k_j-(D_{T_j}B)U.
```

For the homogeneous equation, the source of `d_j` is `-(D_Tj B)U`. Its next spectator derivative is derived by the local weak product rule, not assumed. The original one-step gain and exact diagonal reconstruction then give joint local H² of each `d_j`.

[`GrushinYCommutator.lean`](../../lean/ManyBody/S8/Internal/GrushinYCommutator.lean) proves the actual variable-coefficient commutator and weak equation

```math
(P_0+B)D_{Y_w}U
 =2c\langle Y,w\rangle\Delta_TU-(D_{Y_w}B)U.
```

The positive principal commutator term is expressed through genuine TT diagonal weak derivatives of `U`. Its local L² membership is proved. Applying the one-step gain gives genuine new YY derivatives of `D_Yw U`, hence pure YYY derivatives without any third-derivative input.

[`WeakProductLocalCompatibility.lean`](../../lean/ManyBody/S8/Internal/WeakProductLocalCompatibility.lean) proves local weak mixed commutation and cutoff product rules. [`HigherYRegularity.lean`](../../lean/ManyBody/S8/Internal/HigherYRegularity.lean) uses a common plateau and the previously derived local H² of spectator derivatives to supply TT diagonal derivatives of the same first Y derivative. Exact diagonal reconstruction then gives its full joint local H².

[`HigherDirectionalRegularity.lean`](../../lean/ManyBody/S8/Internal/HigherDirectionalRegularity.lean) proves the spectator recurrence for arbitrary spectator directions and joins the Y and T results by weak derivative uniqueness. Thus every genuine first joint directional derivative has local H².

Finally, the compact cutoff product rule assembles all ordered third derivatives of `χU` from the local H² of those first derivatives and the already derived H² of `U`. The physical wrapper uses an outer plateau cutoff and the actual physical equation. All frozen foundation sources are imported unchanged.
