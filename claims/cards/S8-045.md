## Quantitative proof and quantifier order

For the three true holomorphic budgets `C_j`, set `C0 = sum j, abs(C_j)`. Their actual real coupled Taylor errors are bounded by

`B_k * (2/3)^(N-k)`, where `B_k = 3*C0*(2*exp(1)/radius)^k*k!`.

The helper derives `K = 1 + sum k, abs(B_k) > 0`. Genuine geometric convergence chooses ONE natural `m` with `K*(2/3)^m < 1`. Since `k <= 2`, the actual `N-k >= 2*p+m`. The proved numeric inequality `(2/3)^(2*p) <= (1/2)^p` then bounds each of the nine actual fields at every quarter-box point. No power-series coefficient or approximation assumption enters this choice.

The offset is chosen after the fixed original state, scale and selected chart, before EVERY `p`, EVERY evaluation point and EVERY profile/derivative index. It is common across A/B/H and value/gradient/Hessian at that chart. This does not assert one uniform offset across all scales, charges or states. The cutoff itself is the displayed explicit linear arithmetic expression in `p` and the offset.

The generic source also proves the same dyadic true-derivative bound for one actual complex coupled Taylor polynomial, and a one-profile real restriction. The principal physical endpoint asserts the common REAL three-profile statement. Its retained realness and canonical coefficient facts are unchanged.
