The physical electron number is exactly two and the selected nucleus index is `(0 : Fin 2)`. The inputs are a real unit spectator-center position `t0`, a smooth compactly supported real cutoff `χ`, and its support inclusion in the actual open nuclear chart. For every real charge and energy, the state input is the existing physical graph `hamiltonianGraph 2 Z ψ ((E : ℂ) • ψ)`.

All are chosen before charge, energy and state. The exact public statement asserts existence of `D`; the displayed expression is its definition in the proof. Geometric constants may depend on the center and prescribed cutoff and are not claimed uniform across shrinking unscaled charts.

## 4. Cutoff nesting and quantitative derivation

The encoded derivatives belong to the same actual functions at each stage:

1. `U` equals `χouter` times the physical KS pullback a.e., with genuine first spectator derivatives `d j`.
2. `W j` equals `χmiddle` times `d j` a.e., with genuine spectator derivatives `e j k`.
3. `A` equals the prescribed `χ` times `e j k` a.e., with genuine first Y/T and second YY derivatives.

Both outer cutoffs equal one on an open neighborhood of `tsupport χ`. Consequently the finite derivative chain is the physical second spectator chain there. The output norm sums are for the exact encoded inner cutoff `χ*e j k`. Applying derivatives directly to `χ` times the raw pullback would introduce cutoff derivative terms; that different global norm formula is not asserted.

Writing `B` for the literal coefficient, the genuine homogeneous weak equation first yields `P_B d_j = -D_j B U`. The smooth local weak Leibniz rule derives the source derivative `-((D_j B)d_k + (D_kD_j B)U)`. On the common plateau, the actual inhomogeneous recurrence then yields

```math
P_B e_{jk}=-(D_jB)d_k-(D_kD_jB)U-(D_kB)W_j.
```

The forcing estimate is proved for these literal terms:

```math
\int_{K_2}|P_0 e_{jk}|^2\le
4b_0^2\int_{K_2}|e_{jk}|^2+
4b_1^2\int_{K_2}|d_k|^2+
4b_2^2\int_{K_2}|U|^2+
4b_1^2\int_{K_2}|W_j|^2.
```

True local L2 integrability is derived before monotone integral comparisons. The Moser bound controls the `U` term on the plateau by `volume.real K2 * H`, where `H=MoserCoeff²*‖ψ‖²`. The global first spectator Lp norms control the local `d_k` and `W_j` integrals. The previous original-state spectator budget controls the local `e_jk` integral. Polynomial absorption and the actual inner potential gain give the common output bound

```math

\bigl(\mathrm{coulombMoserBoundCoefficient}(2,Z,E)\bigr)^2\|\psi\|^2.
```
