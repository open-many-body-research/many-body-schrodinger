## Statement

For `Z >= 2`, the same normalized actual scalar Coulomb ground state supplied by S8-018 has compatible literal holomorphic distance reconstructions across positive scales. Choose either selected nuclear index `i`; the comparison keeps that index fixed. If two admissible positive scales have complex distance polydiscs containing the same original collision-distance point `(0,sigma,sigma)`, their actual reconstructed functions agree on the entire intersection of those polydiscs. Their complex germs at that shared point and every ordered complex Frechet jet agree.

This extends S8-013's real physical A/B germ compatibility to equality of the actual holomorphic scalar distance function `H=A+r*B`, including nonreal points of the common ambient domain. No equality of the two reconstructions is assumed. The proof derives real equality from their literal identities for the same original physical `u`, then proves complex uniqueness.

The same original normalized `f,u`, original H2 scalar graph, ground spectral/minimum facts, physical AE/Moser/Lipschitz laws, reality, exchange and O(3) invariance are retained. The selected-state positive Lipschitz radius `R` and admissible range `0 < epsilon <= min(1,R/4)` are retained exactly; this claim does not replace them by a fixed all-state radius.

`r=min(epsilon0*delta0,min(epsilon1*delta1,sigma))/2`.

It does not presume a complex neighborhood of physical configurations.

Norm estimates keep both lines within the actual analytic ball. The real plane is never treated as complex-open.

Each actual distance polydisc is proved open and real convex. Their intersection is preconnected. The shared collision point lies in that intersection by the actual positive-radius and overlap inequalities, so the common-domain equality supplies its complex germ. `Filter.EventuallyEq.iteratedFDeriv` then gives equality of the genuine complex multilinear derivatives at every natural order, rather than a formal coefficient-only surrogate.
