# S8-006: Physical nuclear KS pullbacks have genuine joint local weak H²

**Proposed tier:** L, kernel checked; human maintainer review pending. **Sector:** S8. **Proposed status:** proposed. This submission is not maintainer acceptance.

For every positive electron number, the existing physical spinful Coulomb eigenfunction graph supplies one physical representative whose nuclear KS pullbacks have all ordered second weak L² derivatives locally on the actual nuclear coefficient patches. The scalar theorem accepts a fixed continuous representative of the original physical L² eigenfunction. Neither physical theorem assumes derivatives of the transformed pullback or a transformed equation.

The existing predicate `ProductLocalWeakH2On F Ω`, defined in [`ProductLocalEllipticH2_v1.lean`](../../lean/Foundation/ProductLocalEllipticH2_v1.lean), means:

```lean

Thus all first directions and all ordered pairs of second directions are quantified, including both mixed orders and spectator/spectator derivatives. ## 3. Domain and definitions audit

`NuclearKSSpace i` is the existing ordinary product `KSSpace × SpectatorConfiguration i`. The spectator index type is the subtype of physical coordinates belonging to electrons other than `i`; it is not replaced by an assumed coordinate isometry.

Writing `x = nuclearKSLift i q`, the literal coefficient patch is

```lean
{q | (∀ j : Fin N, j ≠ i → position x j ≠ 0) ∧
  ∀ j k : Fin N, j ≠ k → position x j ≠ position x k}
```

This open patch permits the selected nuclear collision `q.1 = 0`. It excludes other nuclear collisions and every electron-pair collision, including collisions involving the selected electron. The theorem applies across the selected nuclear collision whenever those remaining patch conditions hold.

The proof uses the actual coefficient

```math
B(q)=-8Z+8\lVert q.1\rVert^2
 \bigl(V_{\text{without selected nucleus}}(x)-E\bigr)
```

and `splitGrushin 4 oscillatorBasis B`. It does not replace the physical operator, potential, or ambient space. Equality with the physical L² state is established before applying the physical weak KS theorem; an arbitrary physical AE equality is not naively pulled back through the KS map.

The ordinary product norm is not identified with the joint Euclidean norm. The frozen diagonal-to-joint reconstruction performs the exact `WithLp 2` transport with the existing product Lebesgue measure.
