## 3. Definitions and analytic audit

`hydrogenProductRepulsion` is the frozen integral of the actual normalized
hydrogen tensor's squared norm divided by the physical electron-pair distance.
`hydrogenProductTrial` is the existing normalized spin singlet in the exact
weak-H2 domain of `coulombPartialOperator 2 Z`. No alternative Hamiltonian,
finite matrix or truncated orbital replaces these physical objects.

[`HydrogenProductDensity.lean`](../../lean/ManyBody/S2/Internal/HydrogenProductDensity.lean)

product integrability comes from the frozen pair potential theorem for the
weak-H1 tensor. It then justifies Fubini and the inner integrability used in
the Newton calculation. Thus the Bochner integral's default value for a
nonintegrable function cannot stand in for a Coulomb expectation.

The new cylindrical integration chain uses a proved orthogonal decomposition
of Euclidean three-space, the literal radius substitution, and the exact
height integral. It obtains the Newton radial kernel 1/max(r,s), with the
exceptional equal radii and zero center handled as null sets. The 4pi factors
are derived from actual Euclidean Lebesgue volume.
[`RadialCoulombNewton.lean`](../../lean/ManyBody/S2/Internal/RadialCoulombNewton.lean)
and [`ExponentialRadialMoments.lean`](../../lean/ManyBody/S2/Internal/ExponentialRadialMoments.lean)
